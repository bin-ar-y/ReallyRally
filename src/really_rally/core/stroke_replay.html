How to read the measurements

Elbow direction follows the upper arm from shoulder to elbow. Elevation is above the plane perpendicular to the trunk, and azimuth runs from the arm’s outward side toward the player’s front. A nearly vertical arm has no reliable azimuth. Flexion is zero at full extension.

Trunk separation is the signed shoulder-line angle relative to the hip line about the hip-to-shoulder axis. Wrist speed uses estimated meters relative to the hips. The 3D trail follows the wrist in the displayed space. The video trail uses pixels in the source image. Finger detections are shown only on the recording.

In supported footwork, the lowest observed shoe sets the court height. A gap in foot evidence leaves the floor unresolved. For jumps or unknown contact, choose unconstrained footwork at reconstruction. Playback interpolates between adjacent poses. Pausing or stepping returns to a recorded frame. The human model uses a neutral body shape. Switch to Tracked joints to inspect the landmarks. Dashed segments and curves show reconstructed motion through occlusion. The arm readout identifies each joint’s source. With too little evidence, the path stays open. Stroke review findings use the measured data. The vertical marker identifies the replay reference frame.
